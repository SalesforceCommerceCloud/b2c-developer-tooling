New `b2c slas` commands cover the rest of the SLAS Admin API: register and update tenants (`slas tenant`), manage password action templates for passwordless login and password reset (`slas client template`), configure social and third-party identity providers (`slas idp`), and log shoppers out of all sessions or delete their SLAS records for GDPR requests (`slas shopper`).
