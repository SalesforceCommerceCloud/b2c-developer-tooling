Manage content slot configurations from the command line with `b2c content slot` (list and inspect slots) and `b2c content slot config` (list, get, put, update, delete), including content, schedule, customer groups, and rank. Uses the SCAPI Experiences API (`sfcc.experiences` to read, `sfcc.experiences.rw` to change).
