Configure how often the Script Debugger polls for breakpoint hits and sends keepalives with `SFCC_DEBUG_POLL_INTERVAL` and `SFCC_DEBUG_KEEPALIVE_INTERVAL` (CLI, MCP, and VS Code), the `b2c-dx.debugger.pollInterval` and `b2c-dx.debugger.keepaliveInterval` VS Code settings, or `pollInterval`/`keepaliveInterval` in a `b2c-script` launch configuration.
